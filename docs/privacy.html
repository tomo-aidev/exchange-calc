Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

TripRate does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All conversion history and settings are stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob: The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, and usage data to serve personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Exchange Rate API: The App connects to our Cloudflare Workers proxy to retrieve exchange rate data. No personal information is sent with these requests.

3. Data Storage

All user data (conversion history, currency preferences, settings) is stored locally on your device using standard iOS/Android storage mechanisms. We do not have access to this data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App.

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at getonnews@gmail.com.

# App Store Feature Nomination — TripRate

## App name
TripRate

## One-line pitch
The instant, offline-ready currency calculator built for travelers, now speaking 12 languages.

## Why TripRate deserves to be featured

TripRate solves the single most repeated friction point of international travel — "how much is this in my currency?" — with a calculator that opens instantly, works without a connection, and requires no account, ads-only browsing, or setup. It was designed from the ground up for the moment it's actually used: standing at a register, a money-changer's counter, or a market stall abroad, phone already in hand.

What makes it worth featuring right now:

- **True travel-first design.** Offline rate caching, a 90-day interactive trend chart to time your exchange, and a manual-rate mode to compare a money-changer's quoted rate against the market rate — features aimed at real travel decisions, not just conversion math.
- **19 currencies covering the routes travelers actually take**, from major currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY) to the regional currencies inbound and outbound travelers in Asia-Pacific need (THB, VND, IDR, PHP, MYR, TWD, HKD, KRW).
- **12 languages, reaching travelers where they start planning.** English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified & Traditional Chinese, Thai, Vietnamese, Russian, Indonesian, French, German, and Spanish — chosen directly from Japan's inbound tourism statistics, so the app speaks the languages of the travelers actually using it.
- **Respect for the user's time and data.** No account required, no forced sign-in, a generous free tier, and a single one-time purchase to remove all limits — no subscription.
- **Clean, glanceable UI.** Large digits, currency symbols, and flags make results readable in the two seconds a traveler actually has at a checkout counter.

## Suggested placement
"Apps for Your Next Trip" / "Travel Essentials" seasonal collections, or a Today tab story around travel-season editorial pushes (e.g. Golden Week, summer travel, year-end holidays).

## What's new (if nominating a specific release)
Added Indonesian, French, German, and Spanish, bringing full localization to 12 languages and covering nearly all of Japan's top-20 inbound tourism markets.
